---
title: What I Learned at Speak
description: Reflections on building product at an AI-first company.
date: 2025-03-10
tags: [career, product]
---

Eight months. One design system. Five shipped features. A team that grew from one designer to five.

Speak taught me that speed is a feature. When you're competing against Duolingo with 1% of the headcount, you can't afford to deliberate for weeks. You ship, you measure, you iterate. The best design decision is the one that's live.

I also learned that AI changes the design process fundamentally. You're not designing static screens anymore — you're designing conversations, responses, moments of surprise. The UI is secondary to the interaction model.

Grateful for every minute of it.
