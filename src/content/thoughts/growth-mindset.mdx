Most people think growth = marketing. Ads, SEO, social media. But the biggest growth lever I've seen is the product itself.

At District, I learned that every screen is a growth surface. The share button placement. The invite flow friction. The first 30 seconds after signup. These are design decisions that directly move acquisition and retention numbers.

Growth hacking isn't about hacks. It's about understanding human behavior deeply enough to remove friction at every step. That's just design with different metrics.
