title: My First Design Internship
description: What I learned spending a summer at a startup nobody's heard of.
date: 2024-06-15
tags: [design, career]
---

The summer before junior year, I landed a design internship at a tiny startup in Boston. I was the only designer. I had no idea what I was doing.

The first week, I redesigned their entire onboarding flow. They shipped it. It broke three things. I learned more from that one mistake than from an entire semester of design classes.

Here's what stuck with me: nobody cares about your Figma file. They care about whether the thing works. The gap between "looks good in a prototype" and "works in production" is where the real design happens.
