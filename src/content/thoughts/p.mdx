i’ve been having a lot of trouble lately justifying the game of delayed gratification.

often time, i get sucked into this trap of - “just one more thing, and then everything will be ok.” but frankly what’s happening to me on the other side… is just another thing. 

between work and my relationship i find very little time to be present. these two things often feel in conflict with each other, in this insanely demoralizing negative feedback loop. investing into my relationship feels like taking away from work. taking away from work makes me feel like i’m not doing enough at work. not doing enough at work makes me want to do more work. wanting to do more work then bleeds into my relationship. and all of this constant conflict ultimately reminds me: shouldn’t there be more to life than this?

if i can’t even be present for work or my relationship or myself, who even am i? and what is this constant struggle even for, then? 

i think i’m at a point in my life and career where i feel decidedly lost. i don’t know where i am and where to start. time’s been moving at warp speed, and it seems like this blurry ball of stress and anxiety and jumping from one thing to the next has just become my life. for a while, i would attribute it to being a phase - find external things to blame it on. but now it’s almost been a year of it. this is just who i am, now.

personally, i just feel deeply impatient with my life right now. i want to go back to how it was, go back to the life that i had, where problems were few and far between. maybe it’s immaturity, maybe it’s naivety. i just don’t feel like im on the right trajectory to be where i wanna be when i want to be there. but to get there would entail sacrifices that i am not ready for. so i proceed bouncing between all of these states, never truly existing, never truly being what i consider to be human, and never truly experiencing what i call happiness. 

my girlfriend says we need to find joy in the journey. which i agree with, because what else are we to do? i can’t live just resentful of the fact that im not free or happy. that’s what it means to be truly not be free.

but why does the journey have to be so long?!
