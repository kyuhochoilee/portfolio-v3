---
title: Why I Code as a Designer
description: The case for designers who ship their own work.
date: 2024-12-08
tags: [code, design]
---

I get asked this a lot. "You're a designer — why do you code?"

Because the handoff is where ideas go to die. You design something beautiful in Figma, write a detailed spec, pass it to engineering, and what comes back is... close. But not quite. The radius is wrong. The easing is off. The spacing doesn't breathe.

When I code my own designs, there is no handoff. The gap between intention and execution shrinks to zero. And that last 10% — the part that makes something feel *right* — that's where I live.
