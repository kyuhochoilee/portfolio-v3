title: Building Clique From Scratch
description: What happens when the designer is also the founder.
date: 2025-06-02
tags: [startup, product]
---

Three co-founders. Zero funding. One idea: shared photo albums that actually work.

When you're the only designer on a founding team, everything is your problem. The logo, the onboarding, the App Store screenshots, the pitch deck, the investor emails, the landing page. You don't get to specialize. You get to do everything.

It's exhausting. It's also the most fun I've ever had. There's no approval process. No stakeholder review. You design it, you build it, you ship it. If it's wrong, you fix it tomorrow.

We're on TestFlight now. The real test starts when real people use it.
