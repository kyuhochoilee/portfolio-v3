title: Making an Album as a Senior Thesis
description: How I turned music production into an academic project.
date: 2025-01-20
tags: [music, creative]
---

Most people write a paper. I made an album.

"NOTHING IS EVER WRONG" started as a collection of beats I'd been working on between classes. But when I pitched it as my senior thesis — a full album with visual design, physical packaging, and a gallery exhibition — my advisor said yes.

The hardest part wasn't the music. It was the discipline of finishing. Every artist knows the feeling: you're 80% done and you want to start something new. But a thesis has a deadline. And that deadline forced me to push through the uncomfortable last 20% where the real work happens.

I'm proud of what came out. Not because it's perfect, but because it's complete.
