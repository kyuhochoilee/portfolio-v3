This is the third version of my portfolio. The first was a simple Wix site. The second was a Next.js app with Notion as a CMS. This one? ASCII art, snap scrolling, canvas animations, and way too many hours spent arguing with Claude about internal server errors.

Every designer rebuilds their portfolio too often. It's a procrastination trap disguised as productivity. But this time, I think I built something that actually represents how I think: a little chaotic, very intentional, and obsessed with the details nobody else notices.

If you're reading this, the site works. That's already a win.
